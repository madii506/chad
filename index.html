How stock pairing works

Robinhood Chain puts real stocks on-chain as tokens. CHAD is built on top of one of them.

Step 1 · The stock token

$HIMS exists on-chain

Robinhood issues tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain, its Ethereum L2. The $HIMS token tracks the price of Hims & Hers stock. It is a token that follows the share price — not a share certificate, and it carries no voting rights.

Step 2 · The pair

CHAD is priced in $HIMS

Instead of a CHAD / ETH pool, the liquidity pool is CHAD / $HIMS. Buying CHAD means swapping tokenized Hims stock for it. Selling CHAD hands you tokenized Hims stock back.

Step 3 · The fees

Every swap pays in the stock

Each trade on the pair takes a small fee. On a stock-paired coin that fee is collected in the paired stock token, so the pool's earnings accumulate as $HIMS rather than as more CHAD.

Step 4 · What that means

Volume turns into stock exposure

The more CHAD trades, the more $HIMS the fee side accrues. A memecoin whose fee stream is a tokenized company, not a token it printed itself. If Hims moves, the fee pile moves with it — up or down.

Plain-English version, not a prospectus. Tokenized stocks are not the same as owning shares, prices can go to zero, and CHAD is a memecoin. Read Robinhood Chain's own docs before you trade anything.

Insane Hims lore

The company behind the ticker, in order, from subway eggplants to an FDA warning letter. Every line links to its source.

NOV 2017

Built inside a startup factory

Hims wasn't founded in a garage. It was assembled inside Atomic, a San Francisco venture studio co-founded by Andrew Dudum and Jack Abraham, with $7M of seed money. First products: generic Viagra and generic Propecia for millennial men. source

2018

The eggplant-and-cactus subway ads

It got famous by plastering New York subways with eggplants and wilting cacti — ED and hair loss without ever saying the words. Bloomberg called it "the startup of eggplant-and-cactus subway ad fame." source

JAN 20, 2021

Howard Marks took it public

Hims listed through a SPAC run by Oaktree Capital's Howard Marks, at a $1.6B valuation. The ticker HIMS started trading on the NYSE the next day. source

FEB 2025

The first Super Bowl ad for GLP-1s

"Sick of the System" was the first Super Bowl commercial ever to push weight-loss injections. Two US senators wrote to the FDA within days saying it risked misleading patients. source

JUN 23, 2025

Novo Nordisk dumped them after two months

Novo signed a Wegovy deal with Hims in April 2025, then killed it in June, publicly accusing Hims of "illegal mass compounding and deceptive marketing." The stock fell about a third in a day. source

SEP 9, 2025

The FDA warning letter

The FDA formally warned Hims over claims that its compounded shots contained "the same active ingredient" as Ozempic and Wegovy. source

The liquidity pool is CHAD against tokenized Hims & Hers stock on Robinhood Chain. Trading fees on the pair are paid out in $HIMS, not in CHAD.

No. CHAD is a community memecoin. Hims & Hers did not make it, endorse it, or hear about it. The timeline above is public record.
